brand-film · example film

Brand films, made in code.

A Claude Code skill that turns a deck, a document or a story into a narrated, on-brand film. This one, 48 seconds long, explains how it was made.

English film: captions in English, Spanish and French (French is captions-only). Spanish film: a native narrator, Spanish on-screen text and captions, every take checked by speech-to-text in Spanish.

  • 1,440 frameseach drawn by render(t) in headless Chrome
  • 6 of 6 takesheard as written by the speech-to-text check
  • Captionsfrom the spoken words, with written forms restored
  • Sourcesscene, script, lexicon and lock files, all MIT
